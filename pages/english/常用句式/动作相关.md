- **做某事很快**：be in a hurry to do sth. 
	- They are in a hurry to get back and play with Polly parrot.
- **喜欢做某事**：like/love/enjoy doing sth.
	- Peppa likes jumping in muddy puddles.
- **轮到某人做某事**：it's one's turn to do sth.
	- It's Peppa's turn to hide.
- **擅长做某事**：be good at doing sth.
	- George isn't good at hiding.
- **期待做某事**：be looking forward to doing sth. / be looking forward to + 名词
	- Are you looking forward to the playgroup?
- **为某人某事自豪**：be proud of sth/sb. 
	- Peppa is proud of her little brother George.
