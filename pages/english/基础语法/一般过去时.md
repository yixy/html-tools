- **定义**：表示**过去存在的状态或发生的动作**。 
	- Tom was sad yesterday. am/is was are were
- **构成**： 
	- **主语 + was/were + 其他**
	- **主语+ 动词过去式(did) +其他**
- 句型转换 
	- Jerry was a butcher. 
		- 否定句：Jerry wasn't a butcher. 
		- 疑问句：Was Jerry a butcher? 
		- 回答：Yes, he was./ No, he wasn't. 
	- They were happy. 
		- 否定句：They weren't happy. 
		- 疑问句：Were they happy? 
		- 回答：Yes, they were./ No, they weren't. 
	- I was very happy. 
		- 否定句：I wasn't very happy. 
		- 疑问句：Were you very happy? 
		- 回答：Yes, I was./ No, I wasn't.
- 标识词：
	- yesterday 昨天
	- last系列
	- in+过去年份：in 2017.
- **实意动词过去式的变化规则**：
	- 1. 直接加ed； call--called 
	- 2. 以哑巴e结尾的加d； dance--danced 
	- 3. 辅y结尾，变y为i加ed； cry--cried 
	- 4. 元y结尾，直接加ed； play--played 
	- 5. 辅元辅, 且重读，双写尾字母加ed； stop--stopped 
	- 6. 不规则变化：go --- went；get --- got；buy --- bought； swim --- swam 
- 句型转换：口诀：**实意动词变否疑，助动词兄弟来帮忙。 助动词兄弟一出现，动词立刻现原形**。
	- Tom killed pigs. (实意动词) 
	- 否定句：Tom didn’t kill pigs. 
	- 疑问句：Did Tom kill pigs? 
	- 回答：Yes, he did. / No, he didn’t.
